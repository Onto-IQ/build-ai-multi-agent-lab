## Bio
Phak is a Thailand-based instructor and practitioner focused on applied AI. He designs and leads hands-on workshops on multi-agent systems, guiding engineers, analysts, and product teams through the full journey — from a first prompt to orchestrated agent workflows with clear evaluation and guardrails. His teaching style is practical and project-based: every session ends with something participants can run, extend, and show their own teams. Before teaching full-time, he spent years building web platforms and internal tooling, an experience that shapes his emphasis on reliability, cost awareness, and maintainable architecture over flashy demos. He is an active member of the Thai tech community, regularly sharing playbooks, templates, and lessons learned from real deployments.

## Audience
Engineers, data analysts, and product managers in Thailand and Southeast Asia who want to adopt AI agents responsibly — from startup teams shipping their first automation to enterprise groups standardizing on multi-agent patterns.
